Why this exists

Most home-buying advice quietly assumes two salaries.

Calculators, planners and spreadsheets are built for couples. Buying alone changes the maths: one income for the deposit, one person for every repair, and nobody to cover the mortgage if work dries up for a month.

More single women now plan to buy alone than with a partner. This plan is for them.

Questions

Does it work in Scotland and Wales?

Yes. The cost calculator handles Stamp Duty in England, LBTT in Scotland and LTT in Wales. Northern Ireland isn't covered yet.

Do I need to be a first-time buyer?

No. It suits anyone buying alone, including after a separation. Some schemes it compares are first-time buyer only, and it says which.

I'm not ready to buy for years. Is it too early?

The deposit timeline is most useful early. It turns "one day" into a date you can plan around.
